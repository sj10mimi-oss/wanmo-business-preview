A white acrylic ice bath for premium interiors: flawless hygiene, design and a “wow effect” for homes, fitness clubs and medical wellness centers.

Suitable for private residences, premium fitness clubs, medical wellness centers and spas

PRIME is the model for those who see cold not only as a practice but as part of their space. It supports not only cooling but also heating water up to 42°C, which expands how it can be used — from cold therapy to contrast and recovery procedures. In commercial use, PRIME addresses two key goals: impression and trust in the procedure. The format is easy to sell as a standalone recovery service or to integrate into recovery programs.
