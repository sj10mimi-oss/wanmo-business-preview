CUBE Inflatable Plunge Tub, Beige + PRO Chiller

179 000 ₽

The CUBE + PRO set is a compact professional cold therapy solution for limited spaces.
It combines the depth of the plunge tub for effective immersion with the power of the PRO chiller for fast water cooling. Suitable for an apartment of any size, a balcony or a small gym

Key specifications:

Tub:
CUBE (plunge tub 80 × 80 × 80 cm)
Material:
high-strength Drop Stitch PVC
Chiller:
PRO (55 × 33 × 43 cm)
Cooling:
down to 3°C
Features:
dual filtration system, ozonation, iOS/Android app control, fast cooling
Performance:
cools 250 L from 15°C to 5°C in ~7 h

Color: Beige

The CUBE + PRO set strikes a balance between compactness and high performance. The CUBE plunge tub saves space. The PRO chiller delivers fast cooling down to 3°C, dual filtration and ozonation for hygiene, plus remote control via the app. The set is for users who value stability and control and are ready for intensive regular practice in a limited space.
